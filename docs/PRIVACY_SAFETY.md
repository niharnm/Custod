# Custod — Privacy & Safety Language

Canonical copy. Every surface (website, Chrome extension, macOS, iOS) should use
these exact lines so the promise reads identically everywhere. Keep it
respectful and calm — never dramatic.

## Required short privacy copy

> Custod only analyzes safe signals like tab titles, URLs/domains, optional
> check-ins, and scan history. It never reads full page contents, keystrokes,
> screenshots, or private messages.

Use this near anywhere data is captured: extension popup, scan/check-in screens,
onboarding, and the Privacy Policy.

## Required safety copy

> Custod is not a diagnosis, therapy, or crisis-support tool. It shows digital
> strain patterns and reset suggestions. If you feel unsafe or unable to cope,
> contact a trusted person or local emergency support.

Use this in settings/about and the footer of the website.

## What Custod analyzes

- Tab **titles** and **URLs / domains**.
- Behavioral patterns: tab count, tab-switching frequency, active time.
- Optional **check-ins** (mood, goal, time remaining) — only if the user shares them.
- **Scan history**, to make trends and resets useful over time.

## What Custod never collects

- Full **page contents**.
- **Keystrokes**.
- **Screenshots** or screen recordings.
- **Private messages**, emails, or chats.
- Health **diagnosis** data or clinical records.

## Product guardrails (for everyone editing copy or features)

- Custod is **not an AI therapist** and must never present itself as one.
- Custod must **not diagnose** mental illness or any medical condition.
- Use the brand vocabulary: "burnout patterns," "digital strain," "check engine
  light," "reset," "focus recovery," "early signal."
- Avoid scary, clinical, shame-based, or therapy-like language.
- No fake demo data, fake scans, fake dashboards, or presentation scripts.
